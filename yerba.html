Where It All Began

Yerba maté comes from Ilex paraguariensis, a South American plant first discovered by the Indigenous Guaraní peoples. For generations, the leaves have been harvested, dried, and brewed into a drink shared from hand to hand, a tradition across Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay, and southern Brazil long before it ever found its way into a can.
